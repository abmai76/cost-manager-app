Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Overview

Construction Cost Manager Pro ("the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how the App handles your information.

Data Collection

The App does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information. We do not have servers that receive your data.

Data Storage

All data you enter into the App (project names, materials, labor records, payments, quality checks, and any attached files or photos) is stored locally on your own device using your device's built-in storage (localStorage). This data never leaves your device and is not accessible by us or any third party.

Permissions

The App does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, or any other personal device resources.

Third-Party Services

The App does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, tracking, or data collection services.

Internet Access

The App can function fully offline. When used online, it only loads its own interface files. No data is sent to any server.

Children's Privacy

The App does not collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Data Deletion

You can delete all your data at any time using the "Reset All Data" option within the App. Uninstalling the App also removes all locally stored data.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy occasionally. Any changes will be reflected on this page.
